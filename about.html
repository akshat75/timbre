Timbre is a music discovery world. The premise is simple: music has always lived somewhere. Jazz lived in dimly lit New York clubs. Grunge lived in Seattle basements. Hyperpop lives in fluorescent-lit bedrooms at 2am. Streaming apps give you the song. Timbre gives you the place.

Timbre lets you travel to an era, walk into a genre venue, and discover music the way it was meant to be heard — in context, in atmosphere, in place. Every visual detail of the interface shifts to match the era and genre you are inside.

When a song moves you, you can record it as a Memory — a card that stamps not just the song, but exactly where and when in the world you found it. Your collection of Memories is not a playlist. It is a record of everywhere you have been.
